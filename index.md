# Keepr — Support

**Receipts, returns and warranties, kept for you.**

Keepr reads paper receipts on your iPhone: the store, date, items, discounts, taxes and the total you actually paid, in many languages and currencies. It reminds you before a return window or warranty ends, and shows where your money goes each month. Private, no account, no ads, no tracking.

Requires iOS 26 or later. [Privacy Policy](privacy)

---

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I add a receipt?**
Tap *Scan a receipt* on Home to use the camera, pick a photo, or import a screenshot. Keepr reads the receipt and shows it for review before saving. The + button next to it adds an expense or income by hand. To try it without a receipt, tap *Try a sample receipt* on Home.

**Keepr got the total or an item wrong. How do I fix it?**
On the review screen, tap any number on the receipt photo to use it as the total, a discount or an item. With Apple Intelligence you can also say what's wrong in plain words, such as "the total is after the discount". Every field can be edited, before saving and afterwards.

**Which number is the total?**
Keepr looks for the amount you actually paid: after discounts, with taxes and charges included. It checks the receipt's own arithmetic (subtotal, discounts, VAT, cash and change) to find it. If a receipt prints things in an unusual way, tap the right amount on the photo.

**Which languages and currencies work?**
Receipts in most languages that use Latin, Cyrillic, Arabic, Bengali, Devanagari, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese scripts, in any currency. The app itself is available in 16 languages.

**Do I need Apple Intelligence?**
No. Keepr's built-in reader works offline on every iPhone. On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on, Keepr also uses Apple's on-device model to read store names, items and totals more accurately. Either way, nothing leaves your iPhone.

**How do return and warranty reminders work?**
Each purchase gets a return window and a warranty period. You can set the defaults in Settings and change them on any receipt. If you allow notifications, Keepr reminds you a few days before a deadline and again on the last day.

**I returned something. What do I do?**
Open the receipt and tap *Mark as Returned*. Enter the refund, which can be partial. The refund comes off your spending and the reminders stop. *Undo* puts it back.

**How do budgets and repeating entries work?**
In Settings → *Budgets*, set a monthly budget overall or per category. Home and Insights show what's left. When adding rent, a subscription or a salary, choose *Repeat* (weekly, monthly or yearly) and Keepr adds each new entry on its date.

**Does it sync between my devices?**
Yes, through your own private iCloud when you are signed in. Settings shows the backup status. We can't see any of it.

**Can I export my data?**
Yes. On the *Activity* tab, tap *Export* for a CSV file for your spreadsheet or accountant, or a PDF report.

**Widgets and Siri?**
Add the Keepr widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen to see this month's spending and upcoming deadlines. With Siri or the Shortcuts app you can say things like "Scan a receipt with Keepr", "Add an expense in Keepr" or "How much have I spent in Keepr".
