# Keepr — Privacy Policy

*Effective date: November 8, 2026*

**The short version: Keepr collects no data. None.** There is no Keepr account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no servers of our own. Your receipts stay on your iPhone and, if you choose, in your own iCloud. We never see them.

## What the app stores

- **Receipts and entries:** receipt photos you scan or import, and what was read from them: store, date, items, discounts, taxes, total, category, notes, return windows and warranties.
- **Income and repeating entries:** amounts you add by hand, and how often they repeat.
- **Budgets and settings:** your monthly budgets, currency, default return and warranty periods, and reminder preferences.

This is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, it is also stored in the private part of *your* iCloud account, so it is backed up and appears on your other devices. Apple encrypts that data in transit and at rest. It is never visible to us and is governed by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Turning off iCloud for Keepr in iOS Settings keeps everything on the device only.

## How receipts are read

Photos are read on your iPhone with Apple's Vision text recognition and, where available, Apple Intelligence's on-device model. Nothing is sent to us or to any other server to be read.

## What the app does not do

- No data collection of any kind, personal or otherwise
- No network requests to us, no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting
- No third-party SDKs, trackers or advertising
- No account or sign-in
- No access to your location or contacts. The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to scan or import a receipt.

## Notifications

Return and warranty reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They exist only if you turn them on, and nothing about them leaves the phone.

## Widgets and Siri

Widgets show a summary that the app keeps on your device. Siri and Shortcuts requests are handled by Apple on your device under Apple's privacy policy, and Keepr receives only what you asked it to add or show.

## Exports

CSV and PDF exports are created on your iPhone. They go only where you choose to share them, and where you keep them is up to you.

## Deleting your data

Delete an entry in Keepr to remove it from your iPhone and iCloud. Deleting the app removes the copy on this iPhone. To remove the iCloud copy too, go to iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Keepr.

## Children

Keepr is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data, so it holds no personal information about anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version ships, with a new effective date, and the App Store privacy label will reflect it.
